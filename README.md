# molecules-and-electrons

Energy power comes in two forms the word energy fuses. Molecule power is power over a recurring flow metered at a chokepoint (the rent recurs, the leverage is a tap); electron power is power over a stock and a standard (the capital is bought once, the fuel is then free, the dependence is lock-in). Because molecule revenue requires the customer to stay un-electrified, a fossil exporter has a structural interest in delay. This paper makes that interest computable with a bistable replicator-with-learning model of an importer's electric share, in which the exporter's price moves the importer's takeoff threshold. It prices the resulting harvest-vs-extend dilemma (limit-pricing one's own price wins only when patient and facing fast substitution, so it is a tell), repairs a Romanian hypothesis whose stake was real but mis-accounted (the current-account channel is ~0.2%, the capitalized rent at stake ~$289bn), and shows the delay is bounded by a global learning curve the exporter cannot price. Ships a runnable simulation whose output carries every modelled number.
